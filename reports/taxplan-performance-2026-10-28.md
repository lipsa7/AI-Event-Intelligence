## Executive decision

TaxPlan Advisor has measurable audience demand, a visible sales pipeline and useful search assets. Its reporting does not yet establish which acquisition activity produces profitable customers. Reconcile commercial outcomes and investigate weak paid cohorts before increasing acquisition budgets. Sections 28-31 record the second-pass cross-check. Sections 37-41 integrate all 36 screenshots received on 29 October, updated research and execution conditions.

Organic Search shows strong engagement among the major first-user channels: 682 total users, 4m 55s average engagement per active user and 2.01 engaged sessions per active user. Paid Social brings 1,182 users but only 23s and 0.46 respectively. Later lead reports establish that Paid Search supplies 39 of 73 recorded new lead-users and Paid Social 17; together they provide 76.71% of recorded leads. Strong engagement and lead volume are distinct signals, and neither establishes profitability.

The most important reporting gap is after lead acquisition: GA4 records 73 new lead-users but zero qualified/converted leads and zero reported purchasers/revenue, while the CRM shows 93 wins. The campaign-import report also shows 0/12 join coverage in its displayed scope. Different periods and populations prevent direct reconciliation; these gaps prevent reliable acquisition-cost and revenue-return assessment.

New finding on 29 October: 99.62% of displayed views have no named content group; the remaining displayed label is blank. Prioritise useful service grouping alongside commercial reconciliation. Updated activity counts do not establish improved sales or implemented fixes. See sections 37-41.

Three immediate priorities:

- Measurement: verify the existing lead trigger, connect CRM lifecycle changes and verified payments, and reconcile campaign spend using identifiers and consistent dates.
- Acquisition: investigate the first-user medium 'paid' (5s engagement) and campaign 6977406146903 (26.0% session engagement). Do not label either fraudulent or unprofitable without further evidence.
- Product trust: review the partnership calculator's publicly retrieved rule text, which displays older thresholds. Confirm the applicable year and calculation logic before using it to acquire more customers.

# 6. Repair specification for the delivery team

## A. Measure real outcomes

Use a single internal lead ID and order ID across forms, CRM and payments. Store consented attribution at lead creation, including original source, campaign, landing page, timestamp and supported analytics identifiers. Preserve first touch and lead-creation touch separately. Keep names, phone numbers, emails, PAN and document contents out of GA4.

Recommended lifecycle mapping: verify the existing generate_lead after a successfully accepted enquiry; working_lead when a representative contacts or is contacted by the lead; qualify_lead after documented qualification; disqualify_lead with a controlled reason; close_convert_lead at the defined customer transition; close_unconvert_lead for a closed loss. A WhatsApp click is an intent event, not automatically a generated or converted lead. These event names follow Google's recommendations [S6].

Send purchase only for verified successful payments, with transaction_id, INR currency, numeric value and service items; handle refunds separately [S7]. Do not expect a generic lead-event value to populate purchase revenue. Define treatment of tax, discounts, refunds and partial payments with finance. Distinguish booked contract value from cash collected.

Server events should follow authenticated CRM/payment changes, use application-level idempotency, retry logs and a durable delivery record. Keep API secrets server-side. Use consent-compatible identifiers and supported attribution/time windows; do not manufacture historical sessions or force old CRM wins into today's GA4 conversions. Preserve historical reconciliation in the CRM/warehouse if analytics backfill is unsupported.

Acceptance: a valid test enquiry creates one CRM lead and one lead event; retrying does not duplicate it. Invalid forms create neither. Stage changes produce the correct event once. A failed payment creates no purchase; a successful payment creates one order; retries do not multiply revenue; refunds reconcile. Test consent denial and check payloads for personal data. Validate Measurement Protocol payloads, then check delivery in the correct property; a 2xx response alone does not prove acceptance [S8].

## B. Clean collection and attribution

Restrict production analytics to intended production hosts. Use separate test collection for local/staging environments. Identify staff traffic through verified mechanisms; test internal-traffic filters before activation because active filtering permanently changes incoming data [S9]. A referral-exclusion setting alone does not remove test traffic.

Use consistent campaign tags: utm_source=instagram, utm_medium=paid_social, utm_campaign=itr_salary_2026_oct, plus a stable campaign ID and optional platform. Use these as proposed conventions, not retroactive truth. Test redirects, link shorteners, WhatsApp links and payment return paths so attribution survives. Retain Google auto-tagging where applicable and avoid conflicting manual tags.

Maintain raw URLs securely and a normalised reporting path. Remove fbclid and other nonfunctional tracking parameters only from the reporting grouping after acquisition is captured. Preserve functional parameters, such as calculator mode. Test customer login, service booking and payment separately from staff administration.

## C. Repair the operating funnel

Define qualified lead as reachable contact + relevant service need + serviceability + an agreed buying timeline, adjusted to the business. Require assigned owner, next-action date, created/first-contact/qualified/won timestamps and controlled loss reasons. Separate lead temperature from lifecycle stage and payment status.

Suggested initial operating standard: assign during staffed hours within 15 minutes; no hot lead without a next action; review overdue follow-ups daily. These are proposed internal standards, not researched industry averages. Validate the six unassigned records before routing; do not infer the other 5,695 are being followed up effectively.

# 9. Management dashboard and experiments

Replace the top-level emphasis on all-event counts and a 100% web donut with a business funnel. The web donut means platform composition, not that every visitor converted.

| Management measure | Definition / source |
|---|---|
| Accepted leads | Unique valid CRM enquiries created in period; exclude agreed test/duplicate records |
| Qualified lead rate | Qualified leads / accepted leads from the same matured creation cohort |
| Customer conversion | Paid customers / accepted leads from the same matured cohort |
| Net collected revenue | Verified receipts less refunds under an explicit tax policy |
| CAC | Allocated acquisition spend / new paid customers on a matched attribution basis |
| ROAS | Attributed revenue / ad spend; report separately from margin |
| Contribution after acquisition | Revenue less refunds, variable service/payment costs and acquisition spend |
| Operational health | Median and 90th-percentile first-contact time, overdue leads, documents pending |
| Measurement health | Eligible-event delivery, duplicates, unmatched orders, missing source fields |

Always show date, timezone, attribution model, consent coverage, customer/staff scope and data freshness. Keep first-user acquisition, session acquisition and financial attribution in distinct views. Revenue allocation must not count the same order in full under several channels.

Experiment A: on the partnership result page, compare a generic consultation link with a service-specific expert-review action. Primary outcome: qualified leads per eligible calculator visitor. Guardrails: completion rate, mobile performance, lead quality and complaints. Review tax accuracy first.

Experiment B: compare a dedicated paid-social service page with the current destination, preserving the same audience and offer where feasible. Primary outcome: cost per qualified lead, then paid-customer contribution. Investigate the weakest campaign before increasing its budget.

Experiment C: introduce a clear document checklist and visible next step after a lead is accepted. Primary outcome: proportion completing required documents and becoming paid customers within a defined service window. Guardrails: opt-outs, support load and errors.

Predefine the eligible population, minimum detectable improvement and sample requirement once the clean baseline is known. Run across a full relevant business cycle. If volume is too low for a powered split test, use staged rollout and qualitative observation, clearly labelled as directional evidence. Do not declare a winner from a handful of conversions.

For seasonal services, evaluate customer return at the next relevant filing cycle. For GST or recurring compliance, use completion and renewal by monthly/quarterly service cohort. The observed weekly retention card cannot substitute for either measure.
